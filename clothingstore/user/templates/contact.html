Frequently Asked Questions

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

Our MOQ starts from 50-100 pieces per design, depending on the complexity and fabric requirements.

Do you provide samples before bulk production?

Yes, we provide pre-production samples to ensure every detail matches your brand's quality standards before starting bulk manufacturing.

How long does production take?

Standard production time is usually 2-4 weeks, depending on the order size and current factory load.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, we offer worldwide shipping through reliable logistics partners like DHL, FedEx, and sea freight options.

Can you manufacture custom designs?

Absolutely! We specialize in custom manufacturing based on your tech packs, sketches, or samples.
