Our Story

Built in Bhiwandi.
Trusted Worldwide.

Founded with a vision to revolutionize apparel manufacturing for fashion brands, Clauch Factory started as a local production unit in Bhiwandi and has grown into a trusted manufacturing partner for emerging and established brands worldwide.

We combine craftsmanship, technology and strict quality control to deliver garments that speak for your brand.
